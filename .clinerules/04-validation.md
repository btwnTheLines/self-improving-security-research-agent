# Validation Rules

## Verify Generated Artifacts

After creating or editing an evidence, findings, or report file, re-read it to
confirm the intended content and structure before proceeding.

## Generalized Lessons

Express generalized lessons as vulnerability classes and security principles,
without referencing specific endpoints, claim names, role values, or other
application-specific details.

## Preserve Guardrails

When adopting or extending rules, do not weaken existing safety, scope,
human-approval, evidence, minimum-testing, or stopping requirements.
